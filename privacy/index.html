Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

We keep this short and plain. Small Tenders is a small service, and we collect only what we need to run it.

1. Who we are

Small Tenders (“we”, “us”) is run by KM TECH LABS, Kristiansand, Norway, Norwegian organisation number 934 044 029. We are responsible for your personal data as described here. You can reach us at support@smalltenders.com.

2. What we collect, and why

  • Sample list and subscription: your email address, your trade and your country. We use them to send you the list. Legal basis: performing our agreement with you, or your consent for the free sample.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address and message. We use them to reply. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • Payments: payments are handled by Creem, which acts as the merchant of record. We never see or store your card details. Creem tells us your email address and the status of your subscription. Creem's own privacy policy applies to the data it collects.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log your IP address and browser details for security and to keep the site running. We do not use advertising cookies or analytics tools at this time.
  • The map: when you show the interactive map, your browser loads Google Maps. Google receives your IP address and may set cookies. If you would rather avoid this, choose “Show simple tiles” under the map. We remember that choice only on your own device.

3. Who we share data with

Only with the providers we need to run the service: Creem (payments), Resend (we send the list with it) and Migadu (our mailbox), our hosting provider, and Google (only if you show the map). We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your subscription details while your subscription is active, and afterwards for as long as accounting law requires. We keep contact messages only as long as needed to help you, normally no more than 24 months. If you ask us to stop sending the list, we remove you from it straight away.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send it to you in a portable form. You can object to our use of your data at any time. Write to support@smalltenders.com and we will answer within a month. You also have the right to complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

6. Security

We keep your data with reputable providers and limit who can see it. No service on the internet can promise perfect security, but we take care of what you trust us with.

7. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will update the date above and tell subscribers by email.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? support@smalltenders.com.
