Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

We keep this short and plain. Small Tenders is a small service and we collect only what we need to run it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Norwegian data protection law.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is KM TECH LABS, Kristiansand, Norway, Norwegian organisation number 934 044 029 (“we”, “us”), the company behind Small Tenders. You can reach us at support@smalltenders.com. We do not have a data protection officer because the law does not require one for a service of our size; privacy questions go to the same address.

2. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

  • Free sample list and subscription. Your email address, your chosen trade and your chosen country. We use them to send you the list and to run your subscription. Legal basis: your consent for the free sample (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and performance of our contract with you once you subscribe (Art. 6(1)(b)). You can withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to us.
  • Contact messages. Your name, email address and message. We use them to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying to people who write to us (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Payments. When you subscribe, the payment is handled by Creem, which acts as the merchant of record and is responsible for the payment itself, including taxes, invoices and card data. We never see or store your card details. Creem tells us your email address, the plan you chose and the status of your subscription, so we can provide the service. Legal basis: performance of our contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). Creem processes payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Accounting. We keep the transaction information we receive about paid subscriptions because bookkeeping law requires it. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may log your IP address and browser details to keep the site secure and running. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

You do not have to give us any of this, but without your email address, trade and country we cannot send you the list. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make automated decisions or profile you.

3. Cookies, the map and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. When you show the interactive map, your browser loads Google Maps, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. You can avoid this by choosing “Show simple tiles” under the map. We save that choice only in your own browser (local storage), and nowhere else.

4. Who receives your data

  • Creem (payments, merchant of record).
  • Resend (sends our emails to you) and Migadu (hosts our mailbox and receives your messages). They process data on our behalf under data processing agreements.
  • Our hosting provider (serves the website).
  • Google, only if you choose to show the map.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share data.

Some of these providers, such as Resend and Google, may process data outside the EU/EEA, for example in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on an EU adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete you. We then remove you promptly.
  • Subscription details: while your subscription is active.
  • Accounting records: for the period Norwegian bookkeeping law requires, currently five years.
  • Contact messages: only as long as needed to help you, normally no more than 24 months.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (“the right to be forgotten”) where the law allows;
  • limit how we use your data;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before.

Write to support@smalltenders.com and we will answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to the supervisory authority, Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority), or to the authority in the EU/EEA country where you live or work.

7. Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections and limited access to protect your data. No service on the internet can promise perfect security, but we take care of what you trust us with.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will update the date above and tell subscribers by email.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? support@smalltenders.com.
