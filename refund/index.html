Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

We want you to be happy with what you pay for. Here is how it works, in plain words.

1. Try it first

The free 14-day sample lets you see the list before you pay anything.

2. Your first payment

If the list is not useful to you, write to support@smalltenders.com within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. You do not need to give a reason.

3. Later payments

You can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again. We do not refund months that have already started, but if you forgot to cancel or something went wrong, write to us and we will look at it fairly.

4. How to ask

Email support@smalltenders.com from the address you used at checkout, or tell us which address that was. Refunds are processed by Creem, our payment provider, to the payment method you used. Depending on your bank, it can take a few working days to show up.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have as a consumer under the law where you live.
