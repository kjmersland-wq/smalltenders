Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

We keep this short and plain. This page explains what Small Tenders stores in your browser, what other companies may add when you use certain parts of the site, and when we will ask for your consent.

1. Who is responsible

Small Tenders is run by KM TECH LABS, Kristiansand, Norway, Norwegian organisation number 934 044 029. You can reach us at support@smalltenders.com. How we handle personal data is described in our Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies we set

Small Tenders does not set any cookies of its own. We use no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies and no tracking cookies. Because of that, we only rely on what is strictly necessary and there is no cookie banner on our own pages.

3. What we store in your browser

The site stores one small item in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device, we never receive it, and it is only used to remember a choice you made.

  • st-map-pref remembers whether you chose the interactive map or the simple tiles under the map. It is only saved when you make that choice, and is kept until you clear your browser’s site data.

4. What other companies may add

  • Google Maps. The interactive map comes from Google Maps. It is off until you choose “Show the interactive map” under the list, and your browser does not contact Google before that. When you turn it on, your browser contacts Google, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies or store data on your device. We remember your choice, so the map loads on your next visits too, until you choose “Show simple tiles”. Google’s own privacy policy applies to its cookies.
  • Creem (payments). When you subscribe you are sent to a checkout run by Creem, the merchant of record. Creem may use its own cookies there, for example to process payments and prevent fraud. Creem’s policies apply on its pages.

5. If we add more cookies later

If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask for your consent first, before any such cookie is set. You will be able to say no and still use the site, and to withdraw your consent as easily as you gave it. We will update this page when that happens.

6. Managing cookies and storage

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Clearing the site data for smalltenders.com removes the map choice above. Blocking cookies does not stop the notices or the list from working.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies? support@smalltenders.com.
