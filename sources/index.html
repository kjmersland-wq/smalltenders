Sources

Official, open, and read by people.

Everything on our list comes from official public tender portals that anyone can open. We read the notices ourselves and pick the ones that suit small firms. We do not scrape closed systems, and no AI picks the entries. The original notice always comes first, so check it on the portal before you bid.

EU-wide

TED, Tenders Electronic Daily

ted.europa.eu ↗

The EU's official journal for public tenders. It carries notices above the EU thresholds from every member state, and we read it first. We read and select relevant notices from this open source.
