Public tenders in Sweden for small firms.

Sweden has no single national tender portal, so we read TED for notices above the EU thresholds and pick out the jobs that suit small firms. The national procurement agency, Upphandlingsmyndigheten, explains the rules and where buyers advertise. Municipalities and regions buy trade work all year round.
