Working across Europe

Practical notes for small firms bidding outside their home country.

Public tenders work a little differently in every country, and language is usually the first thing that decides whether a job is realistic for you. These are honest rules of thumb, not guarantees. The tender documents are what count, so read them before you bid.

The Nordics

Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Iceland

Language Tender documents are in the local language. On site, English is often fine.

Poland and Germany

Poland, Germany

Language The local language matters, both for the documents and for the work on site. Plan on Polish or German, or work with someone who speaks it.

France, Spain and Italy

France, Spain, Italy

Language The local language is strongly preferred, in the documents and in day-to-day work. Expect to need it for your bid as well as on site.

The Netherlands

Netherlands

Language English is widely understood, but the tender documents are in Dutch.

The Baltics

Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania

Language English is relatively common, especially in business. The documents are normally in the local language.
